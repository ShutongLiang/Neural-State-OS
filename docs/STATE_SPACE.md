# Direct-44 state space

Direct-44 is a research taxonomy of 44 computational state directions. The
axes are continuous signed controls, not mutually exclusive labels.

## Somatic — 11

`energy_vitality`, `erotic_absorption`, `general_arousal`,
`interoceptive_salience`, `local_sexual_arousal`, `nociception`,
`orgasmic_intensity`, `physical_comfort`, `physical_relaxation`,
`sexual_desire`, `sexual_pleasure`

## Basic affect — 8

`anger`, `anxiety`, `disgust`, `fear`, `joy`, `pleasure`, `sadness`, `surprise`

## Relational — 13

`attachment_love`, `empathy_compassion`, `gratitude`, `hostility`, `jealousy`,
`loneliness`, `possessiveness`, `pride`, `relational_connection`, `resentment`,
`shame`, `shyness`, `trust`

## Appraisal — 6

`certainty`, `curiosity`, `disappointment`, `hope`, `perceived_control`,
`positive_anticipation`

## Motivation — 6

`assertiveness`, `behavioral_inhibition`, `behavioral_initiative`,
`erotic_initiative`, `general_approach`, `persistence`

## Interpretation

- A positive dose moves in the calibrated high-intensity direction; a negative
  dose moves in the opposite direction.
- Multiple axes may co-activate. The public artifacts do not force the state
  space to be orthogonal.
- Reader values are calibrated projections, not probabilities or clinical
  measurements.
- Mature somatic axes are retained for completeness of the research state
  space. The public project does not include adult interaction demonstrations.

The machine-readable taxonomy is `configs/state_space.json`.
